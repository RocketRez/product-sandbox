Membership Photo Review

Based on the Membership Photo Validation PRD, with changes that cut staff review time. Automatic checks catch bad photos when the guest uploads them, so staff only see the photos the checks aren't sure about. Switch review modes under Settings to compare with the PRD as written.

Mode: Assisted

What changes from the PRD, and why

The PRD sends every photo uploaded online to staff for a manual decision. Most photos are fine, and most bad ones fail for the same few mechanical reasons. Software can catch those while the guest is still on the upload screen. That leaves staff with a short list of judgement calls instead of a full queue.

Questions this raises

  • How accurate do the checks need to be before an operator will trust auto-approval? What spot-check rate keeps them confident (the prototype uses 5%)?
  • Will Lagoon accept auto-approval, or start in Manual and move to Assisted after reviewing the sample results?
  • For gate-check mode, what does the attendant do when a photo doesn't match: take a new photo on the spot, or turn the guest away?
  • Face matching against a previous photo is biometric processing in some states (for example Illinois BIPA). Ship it per operator, behind consent, or leave it out?
  • Which checks give a clear fail (always ask the guest to retake) and which give an uncertain result (send to staff)?
